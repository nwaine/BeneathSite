The practice

Write it down while it’s true.

A Truth is that moment, in your own words — what happened, and what it made you see. Not the goal; the thing underneath it. The only prompt is one question.

“Got winded carrying the groceries up one flight. I’m not old — I’ve just stopped moving. I want to be the dad who runs.” A Truth · Room: Health
  • A fragment counts. Write the part you have. A few honest sentences, captured while they’re still warm.
  • Add Wins when something goes the way you wanted — the evidence that it’s happening.
  • Rooms, not systems. Group what matters into Rooms — Health, Work, Family, or whatever fits your life. No blueprint, no correct way to organize a life.
  • Empty is normal. No streaks, no scores, no feed. Write at your own pace.

The mirror

When you want to look back, Beneath pays attention.

Reflect and Try This run when you ask. Recaps are generated at the end of each week, month, and year; Letters arrive by email only if you opt in. The mirror summarizes and notices themes. It doesn’t diagnose or prescribe.

Reflect

Patterns across what you’ve written

A short, written observation of what keeps coming up — across a Room, or everything — whenever you want to look.

Try This

One small thing

Something to act on or think about right now, drawn from your own words. Not advice from the internet — a suggestion from you.

Recaps

A quiet look back

Each week, month, and year, written up for every Room and for your life as a whole. They arrive silently and wait until you’re curious.

Letters

From you, to you

The same look back, written in your own voice and delivered by email. Opt in when you want it; off by default.
